# Tyre ageing and storage

> Synthetic knowledge base written for this demo; not official manufacturer guidance. Always follow your vehicle handbook and a qualified tyre professional.

Rubber ages even when a tyre is not used. Look for fine cracks in the sidewall or tread grooves. To tell how old a tyre is, read the DOT code on the sidewall: the last four digits give the week and year, so 2321 means week 23 of 2021. Many experts recommend a professional inspection every year once a tyre is five years old and replacement at around ten years regardless of tread depth. Store tyres in a cool, dry, dark place away from sunlight, heat sources and ozone (such as electric motors); stand unmounted tyres upright and rotate them occasionally.
