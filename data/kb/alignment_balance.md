# Wheel alignment and balancing

> Synthetic knowledge base written for this demo; not official manufacturer guidance. Always follow your vehicle handbook and a qualified tyre professional.

Alignment sets the angles of the wheels relative to the car and the road. Signs of misalignment include the car pulling to one side, an off-centre steering wheel and one-sided tread wear. Balancing spreads weight evenly around the wheel and tyre; imbalance shows up as vibration in the steering wheel, often between 80 and 110 km/h. Have alignment checked after hitting a large pothole or kerb, and balance the wheels whenever a new tyre is fitted.
