# Bulges, cuts and sidewall damage

> Synthetic knowledge base written for this demo; not official manufacturer guidance. Always follow your vehicle handbook and a qualified tyre professional.

A bulge or bubble on the sidewall means the internal structure (the cords) is damaged, usually after an impact with a pothole or kerb. A tyre with a bulge can fail suddenly and is not safe to drive on. Do not drive on it; fit the spare and have the tyre replaced. Deep cuts that expose cords, cracks that reach the cords, and any visible steel wire also mean the tyre must be replaced. When in doubt, stop and have a qualified tyre professional inspect it.
