# Mixing tyres and replacing in pairs

> Synthetic knowledge base written for this demo; not official manufacturer guidance. Always follow your vehicle handbook and a qualified tyre professional.

Fit the same size, type and ideally the same pattern on each axle. If only two new tyres are fitted, many professionals recommend putting the new tyres on the rear axle to keep stability in the wet. Do not mix radial and cross-ply tyres on the same axle. On four-wheel-drive and all-wheel-drive vehicles, large differences in tread depth between tyres can strain the drivetrain, so replacing all four may be required.
