# Checking tyre pressure

> Synthetic knowledge base written for this demo; not official manufacturer guidance. Always follow your vehicle handbook and a qualified tyre professional.

Check tyre pressure at least once a month and before any long trip. Measure when the tyres are cold, meaning the car has been parked for at least three hours or driven less than about 2 km. Use a reliable pressure gauge and compare against the recommended pressure printed on the vehicle placard, usually found on the driver's door frame, inside the fuel flap, or in the owner's handbook. Do not use the maximum pressure moulded on the tyre sidewall as the target; that number is a limit, not a recommendation. Do not forget the spare tyre.
