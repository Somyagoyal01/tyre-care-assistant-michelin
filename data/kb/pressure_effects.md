# Effects of wrong tyre pressure

> Synthetic knowledge base written for this demo; not official manufacturer guidance. Always follow your vehicle handbook and a qualified tyre professional.

Under-inflation makes the tyre flex more, which builds heat, increases rolling resistance and fuel consumption, wears the shoulders (outer edges) faster and can lead to sudden failure at speed. Over-inflation reduces the contact patch, which lowers grip, especially in the wet, gives a harsher ride and wears the centre of the tread faster. When carrying a heavy load or towing, many vehicle placards list a higher pressure; use that load setting.
