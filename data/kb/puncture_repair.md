# Puncture repair basics

> Synthetic knowledge base written for this demo; not official manufacturer guidance. Always follow your vehicle handbook and a qualified tyre professional.

A puncture in the central tread area caused by an object up to about 6 mm in diameter can usually be repaired by a professional using a combined plug and patch fitted from inside the tyre. Punctures in the shoulder or sidewall must not be repaired; the tyre must be replaced. A tyre that has been driven while flat may have hidden internal damage and should be inspected off the rim. Temporary sealant kits and plug-from-outside repairs are only for getting to a workshop at low speed.
