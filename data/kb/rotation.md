# Tyre rotation

> Synthetic knowledge base written for this demo; not official manufacturer guidance. Always follow your vehicle handbook and a qualified tyre professional.

Front and rear tyres wear at different rates because the front axle usually handles steering and, on most cars, driving force. Rotating tyres every 8,000 to 10,000 km helps them wear evenly and last longer. A common pattern for front-wheel-drive cars moves fronts straight to the rear and rears crosswise to the front. Directional tyres may only be swapped front-to-back on the same side. Check your handbook, because some vehicles with different front and rear sizes cannot be rotated.
