# Reading the tyre size marking

> Synthetic knowledge base written for this demo; not official manufacturer guidance. Always follow your vehicle handbook and a qualified tyre professional.

A marking such as 205/55 R16 91V means: 205 is the section width in millimetres, 55 is the aspect ratio (sidewall height as a percentage of width), R means radial construction, 16 is the rim diameter in inches, 91 is the load index and V is the speed rating. A different size should only be fitted if it is an approved option for the vehicle, keeps the overall diameter within about 3 percent of the original, and fits the rim width.
