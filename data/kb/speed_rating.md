# Speed rating explained

> Synthetic knowledge base written for this demo; not official manufacturer guidance. Always follow your vehicle handbook and a qualified tyre professional.

The speed rating is the letter after the load index, for example V in 205/55 R16 91V. It gives the maximum sustained speed the tyre is designed for: T is 190 km/h, H is 210 km/h, V is 240 km/h, W is 270 km/h and Y is 300 km/h. A replacement tyre should have a speed rating equal to or higher than the vehicle specification. A lower speed rating should not be fitted except possibly as a temporary or winter tyre where local rules and the handbook allow it.
