# Tread depth and the wear indicator

> Synthetic knowledge base written for this demo; not official manufacturer guidance. Always follow your vehicle handbook and a qualified tyre professional.

Tread grooves channel water away from the contact patch. In many countries, including India and the EU, the legal minimum tread depth for cars is 1.6 mm. Grip in the wet drops noticeably well before that, so many experts suggest replacing tyres at around 3 mm. Each tyre has tread wear indicators (TWI), small raised bars inside the main grooves; when the tread surface is level with these bars the tyre has reached 1.6 mm and must be replaced. A tread depth gauge gives the most accurate reading.
