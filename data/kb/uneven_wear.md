# Reading uneven tread wear

> Synthetic knowledge base written for this demo; not official manufacturer guidance. Always follow your vehicle handbook and a qualified tyre professional.

Wear on both outer edges usually points to under-inflation. Wear in the centre of the tread usually points to over-inflation. Wear on only one edge, or a feathered saw-tooth pattern, usually points to a wheel alignment problem. Cupping or scalloped patches can point to worn suspension parts or wheel imbalance. If you see uneven wear, have alignment and balance checked by a professional and correct the pressure.
